Our Story

Established in 2024, Antarbhag Foundation is a non-profit, non-governmental organization registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, and headquartered in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.

The Foundation was born out of a deep commitment to nurturing the core values that drive meaningful and sustainable change at both individual and societal levels.

At Antarbhag, we believe that true transformation begins from within. Our work is grounded in the principles of Inner Transformation, Sustainability, Responsibility, Inclusivity, and Collaboration. These pillars guide our efforts to create a more conscious, value-driven society.

Our primary focus is on cultivating a strong belief system, especially among the youth — one that is rooted in clarity between right and wrong and anchored in universal human values. By empowering individuals with a resilient inner foundation, we aim to build communities that thrive in harmony with each other and the environment.

"Antarbhag", meaning "Core" in Hindi, encapsulates our profound conviction that enduring transformation starts with fortifying an individual's deep-rooted values.

The organization is committed towards promoting the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), with a special focus on those aligned with environmental sustainability and ecological well-being.
