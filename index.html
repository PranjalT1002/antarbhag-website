Who we are

Building change from the Core

Antarbhag Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated towards promoting sustainable and holistic development.

The name, "Antarbhag," refers to "Core" in Hindi which reflects our conviction that lasting change begins with a strong foundation of individual’s core values.

The organization is committed towards promoting the United Nations’ Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), with a special focus on those aligned with environmental sustainability and ecological well-being.
