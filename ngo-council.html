Our Vision

Introducing Sangathan: Our NGO Council Initiative

Building a Collective Voice: A Council of Change-Makers

In the ever-evolving development sector, collaboration often begins with connection. At our organization, we believe that true progress stems not from working in silos, but from standing together — as a community. With this vision, we are proud to introduce an open platform for NGOs across India: a unified ecosystem that connects organizations not for funding or implementation, but for purpose, presence, and potential.

This initiative aims to form a national-level network of NGOs — an evolving directory and a vibrant council of development practitioners — where organizations can be visible, discoverable, and connected. We envision this group as a non-hierarchical, non-competitive collective of NGOs from diverse thematic areas, geographies, and scales of work. The platform is built on trust, transparency, and the belief that every NGO deserves a place in the broader ecosystem.

Why This Council?

In the current landscape, NGOs often operate in isolation. The funding processes are opaque, referrals are rare, and visibility is limited to a few well-resourced players. We aim to change that by providing:

  • A neutral space to connect and be recognized without any financial or operational obligations.
  • A directory of credible NGOs categorized by their domains of work and regions.
  • A platform to refer opportunities to other NGOs based on thematic suitability or geography.
  • A future-ready council that may evolve to raise common sectoral concerns or lead joint advocacy.
  • A way to highlight the strength of India's development sector through collective representation.

What This Is Not

This council is not a funding platform, not a coalition for project implementation, and not a third-party operational collaboration. It is an ecosystem of belonging, visibility, and value exchange — a social capital model built on mutual respect and shared purpose.

Who Can Join?

Any NGO — grassroots or national, emerging or established — working in any thematic area is welcome to join. We believe that size doesn't define strength; intent does. Whether you are a small community organization or a large development body, this council is your space.

Our Framework

Core Objectives

The State NGO Council is an apex body bringing together prominent non-governmental organizations to foster strategic planning, resource sharing, and coordinated action.

Policy Advocacy

Engaging with government bodies to ensure NGO perspectives are included in developmental frameworks and policy creation at state and national levels.

Capacity Building

Workshops and seminars aimed at strengthening the institutional capabilities of grassroots NGOs and empowering their teams with modern tools.

Resource Mobilization

Creating a centralized directory for grants, funding opportunities, and partnerships, helping organizations access resources they need to grow.

Standardization

Establishing best practices for transparency, impact reporting, and ethics across the development sector for stronger accountability.

Collective Representation

Highlighting the strength of India's development sector through collective representation, joint advocacy, and shared purpose at national forums.

Networking & Referrals

Building a referral ecosystem where NGOs can discover, connect, and share opportunities based on thematic suitability or geographic relevance.
