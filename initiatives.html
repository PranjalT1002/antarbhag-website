Civic Spark

Civic Spark is our flagship initiative for mass awareness and civic engagement. Through on-ground activities, open dialogue, and community-driven action, it aims to reignite civic responsibility in everyday life.

From signature drives and awareness campaigns to public distribution drives and community events — Civic Spark channels the collective energy of individuals into powerful civic action that creates visible change at the grassroots level.

EcoSankalp

EcoSankalp is our strategic sustainability initiative aligned with 6 key United Nations Sustainable Development Goals — SDG 3 (Good Health & Well-being), SDG 6 (Clean Water & Sanitation), SDG 7 (Affordable & Clean Energy), SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities & Communities), SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption & Production), and SDG 13 (Climate Action).

Through eco-literacy programs, sustainability workshops, and environmental campaigns, EcoSankalp empowers communities to adopt greener practices and take actionable steps toward a more sustainable future.

View Projects

Collective Path

Collective Path is our collaborative platform for building strategic partnerships with NGOs, government agencies, institutions, and development organizations. Because lasting impact isn't built in isolation — it's built by walking together.

Through joint implementation, resource sharing, and co-designed programs, Collective Path amplifies the reach and effectiveness of development work across sectors and geographies.

View Projects

ThinkSpace

ThinkSpace is our knowledge-building laboratory focused on research, analytical reports, and thought leadership. It serves as a creative and intellectual hub where data-driven insights meet real-world development challenges.

From research papers and Call for Papers (CFPs) to curated blogs, newsletters, and policy briefs — ThinkSpace produces content that informs, educates, and inspires action across the social sector.
