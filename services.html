Your Trusted Partner for Development Sector Solutions

At Antarbhag Foundation, we understand the operational challenges NGOs face — from writing compelling proposals to executing baseline surveys, evaluating project impact, or preparing professional reports for donors.

To support mission-driven organizations like yours, we've established an in-house expert vertical that offers affordable, high-quality, and sector-specific services, tailored for the development sector.

Whether you're a grassroots organization or a large foundation, our experienced team of researchers, project managers, field coordinators, and writers is equipped to deliver on:

Proposal Writing

From CSR funding proposals to government project decks — we help you articulate your vision clearly and effectively.

Baseline & Endline Surveys

We design & conduct surveys with contextual tools and trained field investigators, ensuring accuracy, representation, and insights.

Impact Assessment

We measure what truly matters — using qualitative and quantitative tools to evaluate your project's reach and results.

Third-Party Project Implementation

Looking for an experienced partner to execute your project on the ground? Our team ensures transparent, accountable, and outcome-driven delivery.

Donor Ready Annual Reports

We turn your data and stories into beautiful, insightful, and donor-compliant annual reports that highlight your journey.
